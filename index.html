      What is crypto swap?

      A crypto swap is the action of selling one cryptocurrency to buy another through a single transaction, without using any intermediary currency.

      It’s similar to buying another currency when you travel, like buying pounds with your euros when going to the UK.

      Swap FAQ

      Here is additional information to answer some of the common questions about crypto swapping and how to benefit from it with Venga.

      Choose the crypto you want to sell and the one you want to buy, enter an amount, and review the price and fees. When you confirm, we execute your Swap as a single order, so you don’t need to sell to euros first.

      Swap lets you move from one crypto to another in one step, instead of creating a sell order and then a new buy order. Crypto prices are volatile, so only swap amounts you’re comfortable with.

      You see every fee for your Swap before you confirm it, and there are no extra execution fees beyond the ones shown.

      You can swap between the cryptos available in the Venga app, such as BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and more. Open Swap in the app to see every pair available to you.

      Yes. The price between two cryptos moves with the market. The price you see is a reference and doesn’t guarantee your order is executed at that price. If the price moves beyond a set tolerance before execution, the order is cancelled.
